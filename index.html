11Digital trade and blockchain readinessجاهزية التجارة الرقمية والبلوك تشين

Jurisdictions that have enacted laws based on, or influenced by, the UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records (2017). These laws let a digital or blockchain-based bill of lading carry the same legal effect as paper, a precondition for scaling eBL and tokenised trade documents.

Bahrain was the first country in the world to enact the Model Law (2018), and Abu Dhabi Global Market adopted it in 2021. Singapore, the UK and France have followed, while China's 2025 legislation draws on its principles.

Methodology, definitions and caveatsالمنهجية والتعريفات
  • MENA Logistics Innovation Index: six components, each min–max scaled 0–100 across the economies shown — port efficiency (best CPPI 2025 port score), logistics speed (LPI port turnaround plus a quarter of import dwell, inverted), liner connectivity (log of latest LSCI), air connectivity (LPI aviation partner economies), innovation (GII 2025 score) and eBL law (100 if MLETR enacted, else 0). Missing components are left out and the remaining weights re-scaled. Default weights are equal, matching the score used for ranks in profile, compare and insights.
  • CPPI 2025 scores ports on vessel time in port relative to expected time for ship size and call size; higher is better, and negative scores mean slower than expected. Ports with too few calls are not ranked.
  • LPI 2.0 (World Bank, 2025 edition, 2024 data): turnaround and dwell times are medians in days (lower is better).
  • Container throughput uses each economy's latest reported year in WDI; MENA and GCC totals sum economies that report both that year and 2019.
  • LSCI is UNCTAD's quarterly index; values are relative to the average economy in Q1 2023 = 100.
  • WTO and IATA figures are global. 2026–2027 WTO values are forecasts and will be revised.
  • eBL readiness lists enactments reported by the UNCITRAL Secretariat.
